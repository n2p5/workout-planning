# 2026-02-16 — Hill & Bell

**Time:** ~30 min
**Equipment:** 2x 35 lb KBs, steep hill, yoga mat
**Format:** EMOM → Work:Rest → Tabata finisher

## Warmup (5 min)

2 rounds, easy pace:
- 10 KB deadlifts (light grip, wake up the posterior chain)
- 10 arm circles each direction
- 5 inchworms
- 30s samson stretch (each side)
- Jog to base of hill and back

## Part A — EMOM 12 (12 min)

Alternate movements every minute:

- **Min 1:** 8 double KB clean & press
- **Min 2:** hill sprint up, walk down
- **Min 3:** 12 KB swings (American) + 6 burpees
- **Min 4:** hill sprint up, walk down

Repeat 3 full cycles. The walk down IS your rest. If you're back early, breathe. If the hill takes the full minute, that's fine — just hit the next movement on the next beep.

## Part B — 3 Rounds, 40:20 Work:Rest (9 min)

3 movements, 3 rounds. Work 40 seconds, rest 20 seconds. Move to next station each interval.

1. **Double KB front squat** — unbroken if possible, slow eccentric
2. **KB renegade rows** (push-up position on KBs, alternate rows) — control the rotation
3. **Hill sprint** — all out, walk back during rest + transition

Rest 1 min between rounds.

## Part C — Tabata Finisher (4 min)

20s on / 10s off, 8 rounds. Alternate between:

- **Odd rounds:** KB sumo deadlift high pull
- **Even rounds:** burpees over KB

Empty the tank.

## Notes

- **Intent:** The EMOM builds a rhythm with the hill as a recurring stressor. Part B shifts to sustained muscular work with the hill as a separator. The Tabata is a pure suffer-fest to close.
- **Scaling down:** Drop to single KB for cleans/presses. Reduce hill sprints to 3/4 effort. Cut Part C to 6 rounds.
- **Scaling up:** Add a 4th EMOM cycle. Go unbroken on everything in Part B. Sprint the hill on the walk-down in Part A (good luck).
- **Hill logistics:** If the sprint + walk takes more than 60s, shorten the distance or pick a steeper but shorter section. The constraint is the clock.
